# Solutions Formulas Cheatsheet

## Concentrations
### $$\text{Mass Percentage of a component} = \frac{\text{Mass of Component}}{\text{Total Mass of Solution}} \times 100$$

<br>

### $$\text{Volume Percentage of a component} = \frac{\text{Volume of component}}{\text{Total Volume of Solution}} \times 100$$

<br>

### $$\text{Volume Percentage} = \frac{\text{Mass of solute in grams}}{\text{Volume of solution in mL}} \times 100$$

<br>

### $$\text{Parts per million (ppm)} = \frac{\text{No of parts of component}}{\text{Total number of parts of solution}} \times10^6$$
