# 01 — Types of Solutions

### What is a Solution?

- A **solution** is a **homogeneous mixture of two or more components**.

**Homogeneous** means:

- Composition is **uniform throughout** the mixture.
- Properties are **uniform throughout** the mixture.

### Solvent and Solute

| Term        | Meaning                                                   |
| ----------- | --------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Solvent** | Component generally present in the **largest quantity**   |
| **Solute**  | One or more components present **other than the solvent** |

↦ **Important:** The **solvent determines the physical state** of the solution.

For example, when the solvent is a liquid, the solution is a **liquid solution**.

---

## Binary Solutions

In this chapter, we only consider **binary solutions**.

**Binary solution = solution containing two components.**

Usually: **1 solvent + 1 solute = binary solution**

---

# Types of Solutions

Solutions can be classified based on the **physical states of the solute and solvent**.

There are **3 main types**:

## 1. Gaseous Solutions

The **solvent is a gas**.

| Solute | Solvent | Example               |
| ------ | ------- | --------------------- |
| Gas    | Gas     | Oxygen + nitrogen     |
| Liquid | Gas     | Chloroform + nitrogen |
| Solid  | Gas     | Camphor + nitrogen    |

---

## 2. Liquid Solutions

The **solvent is a liquid**.

| Solute | Solvent | Example                    |
| ------ | ------- | -------------------------- |
| Gas    | Liquid  | Oxygen dissolved in water  |
| Liquid | Liquid  | Ethanol dissolved in water |
| Solid  | Liquid  | Glucose dissolved in water |

---

## 3. Solid Solutions

The **solvent is a solid**.

| Solute | Solvent | Example                    |
| ------ | ------- | -------------------------- |
| Gas    | Solid   | Hydrogen in palladium      |
| Liquid | Solid   | Mercury + sodium (amalgam) |
| Solid  | Solid   | Copper dissolved in gold   |

---

# ⭐ Easy Pattern to Remember

There are **9 possible combinations** of solute and solvent:

| Solute ↓ / Solvent → | Gas          | Liquid          | Solid          |
| -------------------- | ------------ | --------------- | -------------- |
| **Gas**              | Gas + Gas    | Gas + Liquid    | Gas + Solid    |
| **Liquid**           | Liquid + Gas | Liquid + Liquid | Liquid + Solid |
| **Solid**            | Solid + Gas  | Solid + Liquid  | Solid + Solid  |

### Remember:

**First = Solute**  
**Second = Solvent**

Example:

**Glucose dissolved in water**

- Glucose = solid → **solute**
- Water = liquid → **solvent**
- Therefore → **solid in liquid solution**

---

# ⭐ Exam Traps

### 1. Solvent is NOT always a liquid

❌ Solvent = liquid  
✅ A solvent can be a **gas, liquid, or solid**.

### 2. The solvent determines the physical state

- Gas solvent → **gaseous solution**
- Liquid solvent → **liquid solution**
- Solid solvent → **solid solution**

### 3. "Largest quantity" is generally used for identifying the solvent

NCERT says the component present in the **largest quantity is generally called the solvent**.

# 🎯 Possible Exam Questions

### Q1. What is a solution?

A solution is a **homogeneous mixture of two or more components**.

### Q2. Define solute and solvent.

- **Solute:** One or more components present other than the solvent.
- **Solvent:** The component generally present in the largest quantity.

### Q3. What determines the physical state of a solution?

The **solvent** determines the physical state of the solution.

### Q4. What type of solution is hydrogen in palladium?

**Gas in solid** solution.

### Q5. What type of solution is ethanol in water?

**Liquid in liquid** solution.

### Q6. What type of solution is glucose in water?

**Solid in liquid** solution.
